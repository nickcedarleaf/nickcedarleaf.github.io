The Maker

About
Cedarleaf

"I make pieces that feel like they've always belonged somewhere — a windowsill, a wedding table, a mantle."

Cedarleaf Ceramics was born from a love of slow travel and the objects people bring home from beautiful places. Each piece is hand-thrown in my Asheville studio using stoneware clay, glazed in earthy tones, and finished with the kind of care that turns functional objects into heirlooms.

I specialize in mini bottles and bud vases — pieces small enough to group, versatile enough to style anywhere. My wedding collections have graced tables from mountain lodges to seaside terraces, each set custom-matched to the couple's palette.

— The Maker
